# Store Listing Checklist

Fill these in once before submitting to either store.

## Identity
- **App name:** Onward
- **Subtitle / short description:** "Build habits. Track your whole life. Get better."
- **Bundle / package id:** `com.businessescalate.app`
  > ⚠️ This is permanent once you submit. It's invisible to users (any reverse-domain
  > you control works), so it's fine as-is — but if you want it to read "onward",
  > change `appId` in `capacitor.config.json` **before** you run `cap add` the first time.
- **Category:** Health & Fitness (or Productivity)
- **Primary color / theme:** `#0F1117`

## Description (draft)
> Onward is one simple app to build habits and track your whole life — fitness,
> money, reading, nutrition and more — in about 30 seconds a day. Log today, and
> Onward turns it into progress: streaks, a calorie + macro calculator, a full
> exercise library organised by muscle, a personal knowledge library with quizzes,
> a money mentor, and a community to share what's working. An optional AI coach
> reads your real data and tells you what to do next. Make it yours — rename, add,
> or switch off any area you track. Your data stays private to your account.

## Keywords (Apple) / tags
habit tracker, fitness, workout log, nutrition, macros, calorie counter, weight,
reading, productivity, goals, streaks, AI coach, journal

## Required assets
- **App icon:** generated by `npm run assets` from `assets/logo.svg` (1024×1024).
- **Screenshots:** capture from a running device/emulator.
  - iPhone 6.7" (1290×2796) and 6.5"; iPad if you support it.
  - Android phone (min 2), feature graphic 1024×500 for Play.
- **Splash screen:** generated by `npm run assets`.

## Privacy & compliance (both stores require this)
- **Privacy policy URL** — ✅ hosted at `/privacy.html` on the cloud app
  (e.g. `https://YOUR-APP.onrender.com/privacy.html`). Use that URL in both stores.
- **Data Safety form (Play) / App Privacy (Apple):** declare:
  - Account info (username, email) — for app functionality.
  - Health & fitness data (workouts, weight, nutrition) — stored, not shared, not sold.
  - No third-party ad tracking.
- **Account deletion (Apple 5.1.1(v) + Play requirement):** ✅ **DONE** — in-app under
  **Settings → Delete account**. It's password-guarded, requires typing DELETE, and
  calls `DELETE /api/account`, which erases the account and cascades to all their data.
- **AI disclosure:** the coach/insights are AI-generated estimates, not professional
  medical or financial advice (called out for the nutrition + money features).

## Pre-submit smoke test
- Sign up, log a day, see the dashboard + AI insight.
- Log out / log back in (token persists).
- Forgot-password flow works.
- Export a backup; import it.
- **Delete account** removes everything and drops you back to the sign-up screen.
- Looks right on a real phone (responsive, safe-area insets).

## Still nice-to-have before launch (reduces Apple rejection risk)

  stronger than web push on iOS. (Web push via VAPID already works once the server
  env vars are set — see `../cloud` deploy notes.)
